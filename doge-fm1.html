DOGE FM1 — PLAYABLE NFT SYNTH & RAVE MANUAL

1. 10 SYNTH ENGINES & 10 DRUM PATTERNS

Tap ENGINE to cycle 10 synths: FM Keys, Analog, Acid 303, Prodigy, Trance, Cosmic, Diva Vox, M1 Rave Piano, Neuro Reese & Rave Stab. Tap DRUMS for 10 beats including Gabber 170, Jungle Amen & Psytrance.

2. 20 PAD SOUNDS (BANK A & BANK B)

Tap PADS: BANK A / B to unlock 20 live sounds: Sub Boom, 909 Clap, Hi-Hat Roll, Rave Horn, Angelic Choir, Laser Zap, 303 Squelch, Reese Drop, M1 House Riff & Crash FX.

3. AUTO-SAVE & WAV AUDIO RECORDING

Tap AUTO-SAVE: ON to automatically store your loop, BPM, engine, bank & faders locally. Tap WAV to record live audio and auto-download a .wav file when stopped.

4. PC KEYBOARD & MOBILE LANDSCAPE

On mobile landscape, the deck splits side-by-side for thumb play. On PC, use Z–M for keys, 1–0 for all pads, Space for DROP, and Arrow keys for BPM/Octave.
