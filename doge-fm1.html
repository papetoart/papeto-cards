DOGE FM1 — PLAYABLE NFT SYNTH & RAVE MANUAL

1. BPM - / BPM + & SLIDER (75 – 185 BPM)

Tap or hold BPM - and BPM + right next to each other (or slide BPM SLIDER) to adjust tempo smoothly from slow dub breakbeat to fast hardcore/psytrance.

2. PRODIGY [5] & FEMALE DIVA VOX [6] PADS

Hit PRODIGY [5] for a 90s breakbeat + hoover rave riff, or DIVA VOX [6] for euphoric female soprano vocal formants (Aaah–Ooooh–Eeeeh) synced to your BPM!

3. 7 SYNTH ENGINES (INCLUDING DIVA VOX)

Tap ENGINE to switch between FM KEYS, ANALOG, ACID 303, PRODIGY RAVE, TRANCE SUPERSAW, COSMIC PAD, and DIVA VOX (playable female voice on keys).

4. PC KEYBOARD & MOBILE LANDSCAPE

On mobile landscape, the deck splits side-by-side for thumb play. On PC, use Z–M to play keys, 1–9 for pads, Space for DROP, and Arrow keys for BPM/Octave.
