DOGE FM1 — QUICK START MANUAL

1. SWITCH ENGINE (4 FIRMWARES)

Tap SWITCH ENGINE to flash a new synth architecture: FM KEYS (electric piano/bells), ANALOG POLY (warm synth), ACID BASS (glide bassline), or COSMIC PAD.

2. PLAY KEYS & 1-TAP CHORD

Tap or slide across the piano keys (or use Z–M on PC). Tap CHORD PAD [4] anytime for an instant lush Minor 9th chord.

3. HANDS-FREE ARPEGGIATOR

Set ARP to UP, turn on LATCH, and tap 2–3 keys. DOGE FM1 will loop the melody automatically in sync with the drums!

4. SHAPE THE SOUND

Move the 4 sliders (BRIGHTNESS, CHARACTER, TAIL, ECHO) while playing to sweep the filter and add stereo delay.
