DOGE FM1 — PLAYABLE NFT SYNTH & RAVE MANUAL

1. AUTO-SAVE & WAV AUDIO RECORDING

Tap AUTO-SAVE: ON to automatically save your loop pattern, BPM, engine & sliders in your browser. Tap WAV to record live audio and auto-download a .wav file when stopped!

2. REALISTIC FEMALE VOCALS (DIVA VOX & VOX CHOP)

Hit VOX CHOP [5] for rhythmic 90s house vocal stabs, DIVA VOX [7] for a soaring female soprano anthem, or select FW 7/7: DIVA VOX via ENGINE to sing live on the keys.

3. PRODIGY [6] & RAVE ENGINES

Hit PRODIGY [6] for a 90s Amen breakbeat chop + screaming hoover riff, or cycle ENGINE across 7 synth engines with side-by-side BPM - / BPM + tempo control.

4. PC KEYBOARD & MOBILE LANDSCAPE

On mobile landscape, the deck splits side-by-side for thumb play. On PC, use Z–M to play keys, 1–0 for all 10 pads, Space for DROP, and Arrow keys for BPM/Octave.
